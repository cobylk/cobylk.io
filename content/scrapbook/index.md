Three treatments of the same idea: a page that holds paintings, poems, and embedded sheet-music videos in a deliberately disorganized arrangement. All three use the same pool of demo materials (public-domain paintings hot-linked from Wikimedia Commons, public-domain poems, two YouTube embeds), so the differences are purely in composition and behavior.

- [[scrapbook/corkboard|Corkboard]] — taped-down polaroids on a flowing board. Moderate tilts; hovering straightens a piece and lifts it off the board.
- [[scrapbook/desk|Desk]] — a freeform collage where pieces overlap each other. The strongest tilts; hovering pops a piece above its neighbors.
- [[scrapbook/drift|Drift]] — masonry columns with only a slight jitter. The most restrained of the three, closest to the rest of the site.
